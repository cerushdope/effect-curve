What it won't do

It refuses to draw
what it doesn't know.

A confident line is easy to render and easy to believe. For drugs and supplements that work over weeks, there is no honest same-day curve — so it says so instead of inventing one.

Levothyroxine
Shifts thyroid hormone over weeks. The old model drew an eternal plateau — wrong in a way that also broke the time axis.
Creatine
Saturates muscle stores over 2–4 weeks. One dose changes nothing you can feel that day.
SSRIs
Two to six weeks. A single dose does not produce the effect you are taking it for.
Magnesium
Single-dose effects are widely reported but not established. Drawing one would be inventing it.
